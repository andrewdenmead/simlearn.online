Simulations for the English classroom

SimLearn puts a class inside a realistic problem — a product launch, a supply-chain crisis, a public inquiry — and the students have to talk their way to a decision.

How a simulation works

  1. 1Everyone knows something different. Each student takes a role, with their own emails, documents and contacts. Nobody can solve it alone.
  2. 2Talk, negotiate, decide. The group pools what it has found out and agrees on a few key decisions.
  3. 3Write it up. Each student writes a recommendation, and the teacher gives feedback on the writing and the decisions — and reveals what happened next.

Students: your teacher will give you the link to each simulation.
